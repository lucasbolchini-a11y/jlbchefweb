The chef

Rooted in the countryside.
A cuisine defined by fire.

José Luis Bolchini is an Argentine chef based in Miami Beach, specializing in open-fire cooking and private culinary experiences. Born and raised in the Argentine countryside, his story began around the grill, country-style cooking, and traditions shared by the fire.

After gaining experience in some of Miami's renowned hotels and kitchens, his journey led him to Los Fuegos by Francis Mallmann at Faena Miami Beach. There, he had the privilege of working alongside Executive Chef Sebastián Benítez, who inspired him to find his own path and define the cuisine he truly wanted to create.

That experience allowed him to take fire beyond the traditional grill, combining his countryside roots with a creative, elegant and refined culinary approach. Today, through JLB Chef Gaucho, he brings that identity to private events where fire becomes the true centerpiece.

This is more than an Argentine barbecue. It is a culinary experience built around fire.

The experience

How a night of fire unfolds

Cooking isn't hidden behind a kitchen door — it happens in front of your guests, from the first log to the last plate.

Three hours before

Wood first

No gas, no bagged charcoal. The wood gets lit and then you wait. Three hours of real embers before the first guest walks in.

During the event

The grill is the centerpiece

The Álamo 120 Pro comes to life: cross, cage, brazier and griddle. Cuts hanging, vegetables in the smoke, everything turning in plain sight. The fire isn't backstage — it's the show, and guests gather around it on their own.

At the table

Served in courses

Provoleta, choripán with chimichurri, flank steak, short ribs, fire-roasted vegetables. It comes out in rounds, hot, the moment it's ready.

Argentine grill experience

What comes off the fire

A selection of Argentine meats and fire-cooked specialties, prepared live over the grill. Menus are customized to the event, the guest count and the occasion — this is the range we build from.

  • Traditional Mixed Grill Board

    Flank steak or picanha, grilled provolone, chorizo, morcilla, beef sweetbreads, grilled pineapple and pork loin.

  • Pizza-Style Pork Matambrito

    Grilled pork matambrito topped with homemade pomodoro sauce, melted mozzarella and fresh basil, finished over the embers for a smoky, gratinated crust.

  • Mixed Grill Board + Pizza-Style Matambrito

    Grilled vacío, chorizo, morcilla, sweetbreads and provoleta, with chimichurri and salsa criolla. Served with pork matambrito grilled over open fire, topped with homemade pomodoro sauce, melted mozzarella and fresh basil, finished over the embers.

  • Mixed Grill Board + Brochette

    Flank steak or picanha, grilled provolone, chorizo, morcilla, beef sweetbreads, grilled pineapple and pork loin, plus a grilled chicken brochette with bacon, red pepper and green pepper. Served with Parmesan and parsley fries.

Every board is built to order — this is the base we start from.

  • Side of Grilled Picanha

    Tender, fire-grilled top sirloin cap, sliced and finished with coarse sea salt.

  • Argentine Grilled Sweetbreads

    Fire-grilled beef sweetbreads, caramelized and crisp outside, tender inside, topped with pickled red cabbage and served with charred lemon.

  • Grilled Vegetable Sides

    Tomato, sweet potato, corn and roasted bell pepper, finished with garlic and butter.

  • Traditional Argentine Chimichurri

    Fresh parsley, garlic and crushed red pepper, dressed with neutral oil and red wine vinegar.

  • Argentine Salsa Criolla

    Red, green and yellow bell peppers, white onion and ember-roasted mandarin, dressed with vinegar, extra-virgin olive oil, sea salt and black pepper.

Always on the table — on the boards and on their own.
